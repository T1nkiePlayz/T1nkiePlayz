## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a student from **Western Australia** who enjoys turning ideas into working projects. I spend my time **coding, developing games, experimenting with technology and learning new things**, and I'm happiest when I'm building something from scratch and understanding every layer of it.

Right now I'm interested in:

- 🎮 **Game development**: gameplay systems, tools and pipelines
- 🏗️ **Software architecture**: structuring larger projects so they stay maintainable
- 🐧 **Linux & self-hosting**: tiling window managers, Docker, homelab experiments
- 🎨 **Pixel art**: making the art for my own projects in Aseprite
